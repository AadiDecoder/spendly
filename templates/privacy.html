Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Spendly, we may collect:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and password.
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, needed to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve Spendly, including showing your expenses and summaries, securing your account, and responding to your requests. We do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share information only when required by law or when needed to operate the service, for example with hosting providers who process data on our behalf.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including storing passwords in hashed form. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your session. We do not use cookies for third-party tracking.

6. Your Rights

You can view and update your information, export your expense data, and request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal and expense data is removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Spendly team.
